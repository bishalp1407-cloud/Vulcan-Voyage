### 01 / WHAT IS VULCAN VOYAGE?

**Section label**  
01 / What is Vulcan Voyage?

**Definition**  
Vulcan Voyage is a product design studio from India. We work as a companion to people who have a product idea, a product problem, or an opportunity they want to explore.

**Companion**  
We do not simply execute a brief. We question it, understand the context behind it, and help discover what needs to exist. Then we design the product and help move it toward reality.

**Who we work with**

- Start-up owners
- Business owners
- Product manufacturers

**What we can engage with**

- Everyday products
- Mobility
- Furniture
- Spaces
- Systems
- Emerging products

**Role**

Understand the problem → Question the brief → Discover the opportunity → Design the product → Help make it real

**Closing line**  
VV doesn’t exist to make products look better. It exists to find better reasons for products to exist.

**Visitor question answered:** What exactly can these people help me with?

### 02 / DON’T DESIGN THE OBVIOUS

**Section label**  
02 / Don’t Design the Obvious

**Core belief (display)**  
Don’t  
design  
the  
obvious.

**Lead**  
The obvious problem is rarely the whole problem.

**Thinking**

Question the brief.  
Ask why the problem exists.  
Understand who lives with it.  
Find the need behind the stated need.

Look sideways before looking forward.  
Challenge the assumed solution.  
Explore the directions a market is not asking for.

**Outcome**  
Better problem. Better opportunity. Better reason for the product to exist.

**Visitor question answered:** Why would I choose VV instead of a conventional studio?

### 04 / HOW WE WORK

**Section label**  
04 / How We Work

**Intro**  
Creative thinking, held by a disciplined method. We move from uncertainty to something that can exist.

**The VV Method**

| # | Stage | Line |
|---|--------|------|
| 01 | Observe | Understand people, context, and behaviour. |
| 02 | Question | Challenge assumptions and the existing brief. |
| 03 | Reframe | Find the need behind the stated need. |
| 04 | Explore | Generate and investigate alternative directions. |
| 05 | Synthesise | Bring insights, ideas, and constraints together. |
| 06 | Prototype | Make ideas tangible. |
| 07 | Test | Learn from reality. |
| 08 | Evolve | Improve, adapt, and refine. |
| 09 | Realise | Move toward a product that can exist in the world. |

**Closing line**  
The method is one path. Not nine services.

**Visitor question answered:** How would VV actually approach my problem?

### 05 / WHY VV

**Section label**  
05 / Why VV

**Indian at the core**  
India is not merely where we are from. It is a source of complexity, diversity, resourcefulness, culture, craft, climate, behaviour, and changing needs.

**Universal in relevance**  
The aim is not to design products that are only “Indian.”  
Indian in thought. Universal in relevance.

**Built to endure**  
Products should remain useful, adaptable, and worth keeping as needs and technologies change.

**Designed to evolve**  
A product can grow into a family, a platform, a system, an ecosystem.

**Global ambition**  
We design from an Indian point of view for a world that will actually use the work.
