# VULCAN VOYAGE — LANDING PAGE SITEMAP

**Website Type:** Single-page pre-launch landing page  
**Primary Goal:** Establish what Vulcan Voyage is, communicate why its approach is different, and encourage potential clients/collaborators to bring a problem to VV.

---

## HOME

### 01. HERO

**Purpose:** Immediately communicate what VV is and establish its point of view.

**Content:**
- Vulcan Voyage
- Product Design Companion
- Core positioning
- Short value proposition
- Primary CTA: **Bring Us a Problem**

**Key brand statement:**

> Indian in origin.  
> Lateral in thinking.  
> Global in ambition.

---

### 02. WHAT IS VULCAN VOYAGE?

**Purpose:** Answer the visitor's basic question: *What exactly is VV?*

**Content:**
- Short definition of Vulcan Voyage
- What "product design companion" means
- Who VV works with:
  - Start-up owners
  - Business owners
  - Product manufacturers
- Types of product/problem spaces VV can engage with
- Short explanation of VV's role from problem discovery through realisation

**Core message:**

> VV doesn't simply execute a brief.  
> We question it, understand the context behind it, and help discover what needs to exist.

---

### 03. DON'T DESIGN THE OBVIOUS

**Purpose:** Establish VV's primary differentiator and intellectual point of view.

**Content:**
- The obvious problem is rarely the whole problem
- Question the brief
- Understand the need behind the need
- Look sideways before looking forward
- Reframe the opportunity
- Challenge conventional solutions

**Core statement:**

> DON'T DESIGN THE OBVIOUS.

This should be one of the strongest visual and conceptual moments on the page.

---

### 04. WHAT WE BRING

**Purpose:** Clearly communicate VV's capabilities without presenting the studio as a conventional service agency.

**Content:**

- Research
- Lateral Thinking
- Indian Context
- Industrial Design
- Engineering
- Manufacturing
- Brand

**Framing:**

VV brings multiple disciplines together to move from:

**Need → Opportunity → Product → Reality**

The section should focus on the combination of capabilities rather than selling individual services.

---

### 05. HOW WE WORK

**Purpose:** Demonstrate that VV's lateral philosophy is supported by a structured design process.

**VV Method:**

**OBSERVE → QUESTION → REFRAME → EXPLORE → SYNTHESISE → PROTOTYPE → TEST → EVOLVE → REALISE**

Each stage should be presented briefly.

The purpose is not to explain the entire methodology, but to communicate:

> Creative thinking + disciplined execution.

---

### 06. WHY VV

**Purpose:** Consolidate the strongest elements of VV's philosophy, origin and ambition without creating a long brand-strategy section.

**Content:**

#### Indian in Thought
India is a source of context, complexity, resourcefulness, culture and design intelligence.

#### Universal in Relevance
Products should emerge from real human needs and have relevance beyond their place of origin.

#### Built to Endure
Design should survive changing needs, technologies, markets and tastes.

#### Designed to Evolve
Products can grow into families, platforms and ecosystems.

**Closing positioning:**

> Indian in thought.  
> Universal in relevance.

---

### 07. BRING US A PROBLEM

**Purpose:** Convert interest into action.

This is the primary conversion section.

**Content:**

#### The Promise

> We don't want to design more products.  
> We want to design better reasons for products to exist.

Follow with a direct invitation:

> Have a problem worth solving?  
> Bring it to us.

**Primary actions:**
- Call
- WhatsApp
- Email

**Primary CTA:**

**BRING US A PROBLEM**

# NAVIGATION

Recommended minimal navigation:

**VULCAN VOYAGE**

- Thinking
- Capabilities
- Method
- Why VV
- **Bring Us a Problem**

The navigation should remain minimal and should not expose every content subsection.

---

# SECTIONS INTENTIONALLY REMOVED FROM THE PRE-LAUNCH VERSION

The following should **not** exist as standalone sections at launch:

- Full Design DNA
- Separate Vision section
- Separate Mission section
- Separate Promise section
- Separate India → The World section
- Traditional About Us section
- Portfolio section without sufficient validated work
- Testimonials without genuine client evidence
- Awards / credentials without established proof
- Detailed service catalogue

Their useful ideas have been absorbed into the seven core sections above.
