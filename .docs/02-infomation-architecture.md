# VULCAN VOYAGE — INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

**Website Type:** Single-page pre-launch landing page

**Primary User Journey:**

> **Understand → Become Interested → Understand the Difference → Build Confidence → Take Action**

---

### Primary Purpose
Immediately communicate **who Vulcan Voyage is, what it does, and why the visitor should care.**

### Information Hierarchy

**1. Brand**
- Vulcan Voyage

**2. Positioning**
- Product Design Companion

**3. Point of View**
- Indian in origin
- Lateral in thinking
- Global in ambition

**4. Supporting Statement**
- Short explanation of what VV helps people do

**5. Primary CTA**
- Bring Us a Problem

### Visitor Question Answered

> **"What is this and why should I keep reading?"**

# INFORMATION FLOW

The page should progressively answer seven questions:

```text
01
WHAT IS THIS?
        ↓
02
WHAT CAN THEY HELP ME WITH?
        ↓
03
WHY ARE THEY DIFFERENT?
        ↓
04
CAN THEY ACTUALLY SOLVE MY PROBLEM?
        ↓
05
HOW DO THEY WORK?
        ↓
06
WHAT DO THEY BELIEVE IN?
        ↓
07
WHAT DO I DO NEXT?
```

This gives the landing page a deliberate progression from **awareness → differentiation → confidence → conversion**, rather than simply presenting every piece of information VV has.

---

# NAVIGATION ARCHITECTURE

Because this is a single-page landing page, navigation should remain minimal.

### Primary Navigation

**VULCAN VOYAGE**

- Thinking
- Capabilities
- Method
- Why VV
- **Bring Us a Problem**

### CTA

**BRING US A PROBLEM**

The navigation should not expose every subsection. It should help users jump between the major ideas without making the page feel like a traditional multi-page corporate website.

# CONTENT PRIORITY

### MUST HAVE

- What VV is
- Product Design Companion positioning
- Don't Design the Obvious
- What VV brings
- How VV works
- Primary CTA
- Contact options

### SUPPORTING

- Indian perspective
- Built to endure
- Designed to evolve
- Global ambition
- Broader product areas

### DO NOT OVERLOAD

- All 8 Design DNA principles
- Long mission statements
- Detailed methodology explanations
- Extensive capability descriptions
- Corporate-style About Us content
- Unverified claims
- Portfolio/proof that does not yet exist
